Sample Theory Questions

What do you check before starting the engine?
Check the handbrake is on, gears neutral, doors closed, seat/steering adjusted, mirrors set, seatbelts fastened.
When should you use the car’s horn?
Only to warn of danger or make presence known. Not in built-up areas between 11:30pm–7am except in emergency.
What does a broken white line crossing the left hand lane mean?
It is a yield line. You must give way to traffic on the major road ahead.
